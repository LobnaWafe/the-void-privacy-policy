Privacy Policy

The Void

Last Updated: October 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to The Void, a psychological horror game developed by Lobna Wafe. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the game handles information.

2. Information Collection

The Void does not intentionally collect, request, or transmit personal information to the developer or any external server. The game does not require an account to play.

3. Offline Gameplay and Local Storage

The Void is designed to run offline and does not require an internet connection for its core gameplay. Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device and are not transmitted through the game's own functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

The Void does not intentionally use online analytics, advertising services, or remote servers as part of its gameplay. If third-party libraries or services are added in future versions, this policy will be updated as needed.

5. Notifications

The game may include local notification functionality. Local notifications are generated on the device and do not require a remote notification server.

6. Children's Privacy

The Void does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

8. Contact Information

Developer: Lobna Wafe

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the developer contact information provided on the game's distribution page.
